Frequently asked questions

What is IoT development?

IoT development means building physical devices that collect data with sensors and send it over the internet to software that stores, analyses and acts on it.

Which hardware do you use?

We mainly use Arduino, ESP32, ESP8266 and Raspberry Pi, and choose the board that fits your power, cost and connectivity needs.

Can you build a prototype first?

Yes. We build a working prototype, test it with you, then move to a production-ready design.
